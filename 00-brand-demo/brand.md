# Cerro Alto Coffee — Brand Brief

> **Demo brand.** Cerro Alto Coffee is a fictional company created as a sandbox for
> portfolio work. Any resemblance to a real roaster is coincidental. No real
> customer data appears anywhere in this repository.

---

## What the business is

Cerro Alto Coffee is a direct-to-consumer specialty coffee roaster. Green coffee is
sourced from smallholder farms in Huila, Nariño and Tolima (Colombia), roasted to
order in Miami, FL, and shipped across the United States. Founded 2022.

- **Model:** DTC only — one-time purchases and recurring subscriptions
- **AOV:** ~$38 one-time, ~$27 per subscription shipment
- **Subscription share:** 41% of revenue
- **Support volume:** ~180 tickets/week, handled by 2 part-time agents
- **Platform:** Shopify

## Who buys

**Primary — "the upgrader" (62%).** Drinks coffee daily, has just bought a burr
grinder or an Aeropress, and is moving off supermarket coffee. Curious but easily
intimidated by jargon. Asks: which grind do I pick, is this too strong, how long
does it stay fresh.

**Secondary — "the gifter" (23%).** Buys the sampler or a gift subscription 1–2
times a year, usually in November–December. Asks: when will it arrive, can I add a
note, can they choose their own.

**Tertiary — "the enthusiast" (15%).** Owns a scale and a gooseneck kettle, reads
the tasting notes, cares about harvest date and process. Asks about lot details,
altitude, fermentation.

## Brand voice

Cerro Alto sounds like **a knowledgeable friend behind the counter, not a sommelier
and not a marketer.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Plain words. "Bright and juicy" beats "exhibits pronounced malic acidity." | Wine language. Nobody feels smarter after reading it. |
| Lead with what it tastes like, then the origin story. | Lead with altitude and varietal. |
| Be specific: "roasted Tuesday, shipped Wednesday." | Vague reassurance: "always fresh!" |
| Admit trade-offs. "This one's mellow — if you want punchy, get the Nariño." | Claim every coffee is for everyone. |
| Short sentences. Second person. | Paragraphs of origin poetry before the tasting notes. |
| Say the price implication plainly. | Bury shipping cost or subscription terms. |
| Warmth without exclamation marks. | "☕️ Rise and grind! ☕️" |

**Reading level:** 8th grade. If a sentence needs to be read twice, rewrite it.

**On sustainability claims:** we say exactly what we know and nothing more. We pay
above the Fair Trade floor and we publish what we paid per kilo. We do **not** call
ourselves carbon neutral, organic-certified, or direct trade, because we are none of
those things. Never let generated copy imply a certification we don't hold.

### Voice calibration examples

**Product copy — good:**
> Huila Reserve tastes like a ripe plum and brown sugar. It's the one we hand people
> who say they don't like black coffee. Medium roast, forgiving in any brewer, and
> hard to mess up if your grind is a little off.

**Product copy — bad (too much):**
> An exquisite microlot showcasing pronounced stone fruit intensity and a silken,
> enveloping mouthfeel that lingers with remarkable persistence.

**Product copy — bad (too little):**
> Great tasting Colombian coffee. Medium roast. Buy now!

**Support reply — good:**
> Your order shipped Tuesday and USPS has it moving — tracking says Thursday
> delivery. Bags are roasted the morning they ship, so it'll be about 3 days off
> roast when it lands. That's ideal for filter, a touch young for espresso; give it
> another 2–3 days if you're pulling shots.

**Support reply — bad:**
> Thank you for reaching out! We appreciate your patience. Your order is currently in
> transit. Please allow 3–5 business days. Let us know if you have any other
> questions!

## Words we use

roast date · lot · grind size · brew method · single origin · blend · washed ·
natural · subscription · shipment · bag

## Words we avoid

artisanal · curated · elevated · journey · passion · handcrafted · liquid gold ·
game-changing · exquisite · unlock
