# Returns & Refunds

Coffee is a perishable food product, so our policy is different from a normal
returns policy. Read the section that matches your situation.

## If something is wrong with the coffee

**We refund or replace, no return needed.** This covers:

- Wrong item or wrong grind shipped
- Bag arrived damaged, split, or with a broken one-way valve
- Coffee is stale, sour, or clearly defective
- Roast date on the bag is more than **14 days** before delivery

Contact us within **30 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo of the
bag label. We don't ask you to ship anything back — mailing coffee across the country
to be thrown away helps nobody.

Refunds go to the original payment method and post in **5–10 business days**
depending on your bank.

## If you just don't like it

We'll make it right once. Tell us what you didn't like about it and we'll either send
a different coffee that suits your taste better, or refund that bag — **your choice**,
one time per customer.

Beyond that first time, we can't refund opened bags for preference. We'd rather help
you pick correctly than keep replacing bags: message us with your brewer and how you
take it, and we'll point you at the right roast.

**Unopened** bags can be refunded within **30 days of delivery**, no reason required.
Return shipping is on you unless the error was ours.

## Equipment

Unopened and unused equipment: **30 days**, full refund, you pay return shipping.
Opened or used equipment can only be returned if it's defective, in which case we
cover shipping both ways. Manufacturer warranties (grinders, scales) are handled by
the manufacturer — we'll give you the contact and the proof of purchase.

## Subscriptions

- A shipment that has **not yet been roasted** can be cancelled for a full refund
- A shipment that has **already shipped** can't be cancelled, but the coffee-quality
  rules above still apply
- We don't charge cancellation fees and we don't require a minimum number of shipments

See the subscription policy for pausing, skipping and frequency changes.

## Gift orders

The gift recipient can request a replacement or an exchange directly — they don't
need the purchaser's permission and we won't tell the purchaser unless asked.
**Refunds go to the original payment method only**, which means the purchaser. We
can't refund a gift to the recipient.

## What we don't refund

- Shipping charges on a returned order, unless we made the error
- Orders reported more than **30 days** after delivery
- Coffee bought through a third-party retailer — take those to the retailer

## Chargebacks

If you open a chargeback we can't keep working the case with you; the bank takes over
and it takes 60–90 days. Message us first — we're faster and we say yes more often
than a bank does.
