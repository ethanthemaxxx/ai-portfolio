# Choosing a Grind & Brewing Guide

The single most common support question we get is "which grind do I pick?" Here's the
whole answer.

## Pick your grind by your brewer

| Your brewer | Choose | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso machine | **Espresso** | Powdered sugar |
| Moka pot / stovetop | **Espresso** | Powdered sugar |
| Aeropress | **Filter** | Table salt |
| V60, Chemex, pour-over | **Filter** | Table salt |
| Drip machine (Mr. Coffee, Technivorm) | **Filter** | Table salt |
| French press | **French Press** | Coarse sea salt |
| Cold brew | **French Press** | Coarse sea salt |
| Percolator | **French Press** | Coarse sea salt |
| You own a grinder | **Whole Bean** | — |

If you're between two, choose the **finer** one. Coffee that's too coarse tastes weak
and sour; too fine tastes bitter but at least tastes like something.

**Nespresso and Keurig machines take proprietary pods. Our coffee doesn't work in
them** unless you have a refillable capsule, in which case order Espresso grind.

## Which coffee should I get?

- **New to specialty coffee, drink it black or with a splash:** Huila Reserve
- **You take it with milk or make lattes:** Volcán Dark
- **You want fruit and acidity, you brew pour-over:** Nariño Microlot
- **You want something easy that works everywhere:** Casa Blend
- **Evening or caffeine-sensitive:** Sereno Decaf (sugarcane process, not chemical)

## Basic recipes

**Pour-over (V60, 1 cup)**
22 g coffee, filter grind, 360 g water at 96 °C. Bloom with 60 g for 40 seconds, then
pour in stages to 360 g. Done around 2:45.

**French press (1 L)**
60 g coffee, coarse grind, 900 g water just off boil. Stir at 1 minute, wait to 4
minutes, press slowly, decant immediately so it doesn't keep extracting.

**Aeropress (1 cup)**
15 g coffee, filter grind, 220 g water at 90 °C. Stir 10 seconds, steep 1:30, press for
30 seconds.

**Espresso**
18 g in, 36 g out, 25–30 seconds. If it runs fast and tastes sour, grind finer. If it
chokes and tastes bitter, grind coarser. Change one thing at a time.

## Troubleshooting

| It tastes… | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Sour, thin, empty | Under-extracted | Grind finer, hotter water, or brew longer |
| Bitter, dry, harsh | Over-extracted | Grind coarser, cooler water, or brew shorter |
| Weak but not sour | Not enough coffee | Raise the dose, keep the ratio |
| Muddy, silty | Grind too fine for the brewer | Go coarser |
| Flat, papery | Age | Check the roast date |

Standard ratio to start from: **1 g coffee to 16 g water**. Weigh it once and you'll
never guess again — a kitchen scale is the cheapest upgrade in coffee.

## Caffeine

A 250 g bag makes roughly 14 cups at our standard ratio. A brewed cup is around
95–120 mg of caffeine; espresso is about 65 mg per shot. Decaf is not caffeine-free —
it retains roughly **2–3%** of the original caffeine, about 3 mg per cup.

> We can tell you what's in the coffee. We can't tell you how much caffeine is right
> for you — if you have a medical reason to limit caffeine, that's a conversation for
> your doctor, not for us.
