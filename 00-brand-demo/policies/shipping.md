# Shipping & Delivery

## Roast-to-ship schedule

We roast **Mondays and Thursdays** and every bag ships the same day it's roasted.
Your coffee is never sitting in a warehouse.

- Order by **11:00 AM ET Sunday** → roasted and shipped **Monday**
- Order by **11:00 AM ET Wednesday** → roasted and shipped **Thursday**
- Orders placed after a cut-off go into the next roast

This means the longest you'll ever wait before your order ships is **4 days**, and
that only happens if you order Sunday afternoon.

Equipment-only orders (grinders, drippers, filters, mugs) don't wait for a roast day.
They ship **within 1 business day**. If an order contains both coffee and equipment,
the whole order ships together on the roast day unless you contact us to split it.

## Transit times

Transit is counted from the ship date, not the order date.

| Destination | Standard (USPS Ground Advantage) | Express (UPS 2-Day) |
|---|---|---|
| FL, GA, AL, SC | 1–2 business days | 2 business days |
| Eastern & Midwest US | 2–4 business days | 2 business days |
| Mountain & Pacific US | 4–6 business days | 2 business days |
| AK, HI | 6–9 business days | not available |

## Shipping cost

- **Free standard shipping on orders of $45 or more** (subtotal after discounts, before tax)
- Standard shipping under $45: **flat $6.50**
- Express (UPS 2-Day): **$14.95**, available to the contiguous 48 states only
- Subscriptions ship free at every order value, at every frequency

## Where we ship

All **50 US states**, plus **DC** and **Puerto Rico**. PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses
are supported on standard shipping only.

We do **not** ship internationally. There is no workaround, freight forwarder
arrangement, or exception process.

## Tracking

Tracking is emailed the moment the label is scanned, usually between 5:00 and 7:00 PM
ET on the ship day. It can take USPS up to 24 hours to show the first movement scan —
a tracking number that says "label created" the next morning is normal and not a sign
of a problem.

## Late, lost and stolen packages

- **Delayed in transit:** if tracking hasn't moved in **5 business days**, contact us
  and we'll open a carrier trace and reship at no cost. We don't make you wait for the
  trace to close.
- **Marked delivered but missing:** check with neighbors and your building first, then
  contact us within **7 days** of the delivery scan. We reship once per address at no
  charge.
- **Damaged in transit:** send a photo of the box and the bag. We reship immediately.
  Don't return the damaged item — keep it or compost the coffee.

## Address changes

We can change an address only **before the roast-day cut-off**. Once a label is
printed we can't intercept it. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable we'll
refund the coffee but not the original shipping.

## Weather and carrier holidays

We don't ship on federal holidays. During hurricane season (June–November) Florida
closures can push a roast day by 24–48 hours; we email everyone affected before the
scheduled ship date.
