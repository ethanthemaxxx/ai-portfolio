# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Where is my order?**
Tracking is emailed on the ship day. If it's been more than 5 business days with no
movement, contact us and we'll open a trace and reship.

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Yes, until the roast-day cut-off (11:00 AM ET the day before roasting). After that the
bag is roasted and labelled.

**Can I add to an existing order?**
Only before the cut-off, and only by messaging us — the store can't merge orders
automatically.

**Do you offer gift notes?**
Yes. There's a note field at checkout, printed on a card, no pricing shown on the
packing slip for gift orders.

## Product

**Is your coffee organic?**
No. We are not certified organic and we don't claim to be. Several of our producers
farm without synthetic inputs, but without certification we won't put the word on a bag.

**Is it fair trade?**
Not certified. We publish what we pay per kilo of green coffee on each product page,
which is above the Fair Trade floor. We think the number is more useful than the label.

**Do you sell decaf?**
Yes — Sereno Decaf, sugarcane (ethyl acetate) process. No methylene chloride.

**Are your bags recyclable?**
The bags are multi-layer and are **not** curbside recyclable. The valve and the tin tie
are the problem. We're working on it and we're not going to pretend otherwise.

**Do you sell whole bean and ground?**
Both. Whole bean if you have a grinder; otherwise pick your grind by brewer — see the
brewing guide.

## Account & payment

**What payment methods do you take?**
All major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay. No PayPal, no crypto, no purchase
orders.

**Do you store my card?**
Card details are held by Shopify Payments. We never see or store full card numbers.

**Can I use two discount codes?**
No — one code per order. The subscriber discount applies automatically on top of a code.

## Shipping

**Do you ship internationally?**
No. US, DC and Puerto Rico only.

**How fast is shipping?**
1–2 days in the Southeast, up to 6 days on the West Coast, counted from the ship day.
Express is available in the contiguous 48.

**Is shipping free?**
Free over $45, and always free on subscriptions.

## Things we can't answer here

The following need a human. If you're asking about one of these, we'll connect you with
someone rather than guess:

- **Wholesale, café accounts, and bulk pricing** — we do have a wholesale program, but
  terms, minimums and pricing are handled case by case by our accounts team.
- **Corporate gifting and custom/private label** — same, handled individually.
- **Press, partnerships, and collaborations.**
- **Anything medical** — caffeine and health conditions, pregnancy, medication
  interactions, allergen safety beyond what's on the label.
- **Legal requests, chargebacks in progress, and disputes.**
- **Refunds above $150**, which need a supervisor's approval.
- **Any request to change an order that has already shipped.**
