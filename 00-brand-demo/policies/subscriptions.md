# Subscriptions

## How it works

Pick a coffee, a grind, a size and a frequency. We roast and ship on your schedule
and charge the card on file the morning of the roast day.

**Frequencies:** every 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 weeks.

**Subscriber benefits:**
- **10% off** every shipment, forever, stacked on top of any size discount
- **Free shipping** at every order value
- First access to microlots before they go on the public site
- No minimum commitment, no cancellation fee

## Managing your subscription

Everything is self-serve in your account under **Manage subscription**:

| Action | Deadline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Skip next shipment | before the charge | Skipping doesn't shift your schedule |
| Pause | before the charge | Pause for 2, 4, 8 weeks or indefinitely |
| Change coffee, grind or size | before the charge | Price adjusts automatically |
| Change frequency | any time | New frequency starts after the next shipment |
| Change address | before the roast-day cut-off | Same as one-time orders |
| Cancel | any time | Takes effect immediately |

**The charge happens at 6:00 AM ET on the roast day** (Monday or Thursday). Any change
made before that moment applies to that shipment. After it, the shipment is locked.

If you miss the window, message us the same morning — if it hasn't been roasted yet
we can usually still catch it. That's a courtesy, not a guarantee.

## Failed payments

If the card declines we retry on **day 1, day 3 and day 7**. You get an email each
time with a link to update the card. After the third failure the subscription pauses
automatically — it is **not** cancelled, and your discount and position are kept. Fix
the card and it resumes on the next roast day.

## Gift subscriptions

Prepaid for 3, 6 or 12 shipments. The recipient gets an email on the date you pick
and can choose their own coffee and grind from what you prepaid. Gift subscriptions
don't auto-renew — nobody gets a surprise charge. When the shipments run out we email
the recipient once, and that's the end of it unless they subscribe themselves.

## Price changes

If we have to raise prices we email every active subscriber **30 days ahead**. Your
current price holds for any shipment already scheduled inside that window.

## What subscriptions don't cover

Equipment can't be put on a subscription. Decaf is available on subscription, but
single-origin microlots are not — they sell out, and we won't promise a bag we can't
guarantee we'll have.
