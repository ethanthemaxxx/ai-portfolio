## The descriptions this came from

### Tolima Sunrise (2025 harvest, sold out)

*Retired lot. The merchant's favourite piece of their own copy.*

> Tolima Sunrise tastes like red grape and cocoa. It is the bag we reached for on a slow morning.
> Medium roast, washed, and forgiving if your grind is a little coarse.
> You can brew it in a dripper or a French press and it holds up either way.
> We roasted it Monday and shipped it the same day.

### Huila Late Harvest (2025, sold out)

*Retired lot. Shows how the brand says 'this one is not for everyone'.*

> Huila Late Harvest is bright without being sharp. Think green apple, honey and a little black tea.
> It is a light roast, so grind finer than you think and use water just off the boil. If you want a mellow cup, this is not your bag.
> We paid $7.10 per kilo for the green coffee.

### Steel Tamper 58 mm (discontinued)

*Discontinued accessory. Shows the voice with no coffee facts to lean on.*

> This is a plain steel tamper, 58 mm across. It fits most home espresso baskets.
> The handle is walnut, so it warms up in your hand instead of staying cold.
> Nothing here is clever. It presses coffee flat, which is the whole job.

### Holiday Pair (seasonal, 2025)

*Seasonal box. Shows how the brand writes for the gift buyer.*

> The Holiday Pair is two 250 g bags in one box, with a card you can write on.
> Pick any two coffees on the site and we grind them the way you ask.
> It ships in one parcel, so you pay shipping once. If they end up hating one bag, tell us and we swap it.

### Subscription (evergreen page copy)

*Page copy, not a product. Included because it is the clearest example of the voice.*

> A subscription means we roast for you first. You pick the size, the grind and how often.
> Every shipment is roasted the morning it goes out, and you can skip or cancel from your account.
> Most people start with one bag every two weeks and adjust after a month.
